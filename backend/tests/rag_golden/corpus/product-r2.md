# Acme R2 Warehouse Robot

## Specifications
The R2 carries a payload of up to 12 kg. Its battery lasts 8 hours on a full charge, and a full charge takes 90 minutes. Maximum speed is 1.5 metres per second.

## Error codes
E-4012 means the battery is overheating: stop the robot and let it cool for 20 minutes.
E-2207 means a lidar sensor fault: clean the sensor window and restart.
E-1150 means the wheel motor is jammed: check for debris under the chassis.

## Maintenance
Replace the drive wheels every 2,000 operating hours. Firmware updates are installed automatically each Sunday at 02:00.
