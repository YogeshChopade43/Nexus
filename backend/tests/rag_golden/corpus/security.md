# Information Security Rules

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and the HR portal.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost laptop or phone to security@acme.example within 2 hours. The device is wiped remotely once the report is confirmed.

## Office access
Badge access to the offices works from 7am to 10pm on weekdays. Weekend access needs approval from Facilities.

## Incidents
Phishing emails are reported with the Report button in the mail client. Serious incidents are tracked under code SEC-9001.
