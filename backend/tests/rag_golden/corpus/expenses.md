# Travel and Expenses

## Meals
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 1,500 rupees. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Claims
Submit receipts within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are rejected. Your line manager approves each claim.

## Mileage
Using your own car for work trips is reimbursed at 12 rupees per kilometre.

## Expense codes
Use code EXP-114 for travel, EXP-207 for client entertainment and EXP-310 for training courses.
