# Shipping

## Domestic delivery
Orders within India arrive in 3 to 5 business days. Deliveries to Pune arrive in 2 business days because they ship from our Hinjewadi warehouse.

## International delivery
International orders take 10 to 14 business days. Customs duties are paid by the customer.

## Costs and tracking
Shipping is free for orders over 5,000 rupees. Every order gets a tracking link by SMS once it leaves the warehouse.
