# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Damaged items must be reported with a photo within 48 hours of delivery.

## Fees
Opened items carry a 10 percent restocking fee. Unopened items are refunded in full.

## Refund timing
Refunds are processed within 7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Money goes back to the original payment method.
