# Acme Robotics Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every employee gets 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; the rest expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected birth date.

## How to request leave
Requests go through the Harbor HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Your line manager approves or declines within 3 working days.
