# Onboarding Guide

## Before your first day
IT prepares your laptop three days before you start. You receive your badge at reception on day one.

## First week
Orientation starts on Monday at 10:00 in the Pune headquarters. Every new joiner gets a buddy from another team for their first 60 days.

## Probation
The probation period lasts six months. A review with your manager happens at the end of month three and month six.
